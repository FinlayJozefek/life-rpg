Armour glow

Every piece of armour glows in its own rarity colour, from Rare up: blue for Rare, purple for Epic, gold for Legendary, red for Mythic. A full Epic set glows purple all over; one Mythic crown glows red round your head. Legendary and up pulse, Mythic gives off embers and Divine (only the Wings of the Seraph) sparkles. Common and Uncommon don't glow. These are the real sets, only up to the rarities they actually have.
